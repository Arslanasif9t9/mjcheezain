Terms & Policies (Simplified)

Welcome to the MJCheezain Seller Community!

Before you start selling on our platform, please read and understand the following terms and policies. These are designed to protect both you (the seller) and our buyers, and to make sure everything runs smoothly.

1. Who Can Become a Seller?

Anyone can join MJCheezain as a seller — whether you're:

  • An individual
  • A shop owner
  • A wholesaler
  • Or a brand

You just need to:

  • Register with correct details
  • Upload real and legal products
  • Follow our seller rules

2. Product Rules (What You Can Sell)

We want to keep MJCheezain safe and trusted for buyers. That's why we don't allow:

  • Fake or duplicate items
  • Banned, stolen, or illegal products
  • Expired or damaged goods

Only original, working, and clearly described products are allowed.

3. Free Listings & Commission Policy

We support new sellers by offering:

  • First 5 product listings absolutely FREE
  • After that, only 5% commission per sale
  • There are no extra setup fees. We only earn when you earn.

Example:

If your product sells for Rs. 1,000 — you keep Rs. 950, and MJCheezain takes Rs. 50 as commission.

4. Shipping Rules

To keep customers happy, you must:

  • Ship products on time (within 1–2 working days)
  • Use proper packaging
  • Add correct tracking info

Late or no shipping may result in order cancellation or penalties.

5. Returns & Complaints

If a customer receives a:

  • Wrong item
  • Broken/damaged product
  • Fake product

You must accept the return and cooperate with our team.

If you repeat such issues, your seller account may be paused or banned.

6. Seller Responsibility

As a seller on MJCheezain, you're responsible for:

  • Keeping your prices and stock updated
  • Providing real product information
  • Responding to queries if needed
  • Ensuring customer satisfaction

We promote top-rated sellers with better visibility and more orders.

Seller Agreement

By registering as a seller on MJCheezain, you agree to all the terms and policies mentioned above. We reserve the right to update these policies with prior notice to sellers.
