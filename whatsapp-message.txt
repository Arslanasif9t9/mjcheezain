Website ka poora check-up (customer, seller aur admin — teeno side) kiya hai. Jo jo masle site use karte hue samne aate hain, wo simple lafzon mein neeche likhe hain. Technical detail nahi, sirf "kahan dikkat hoti hai" — takay aap samajh sakein.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
🔴 BADE MASLE (launch se pehle theek hone chahiye)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1) Jis product ki "original price (MRP)" na dali ho, us product ka page bilkul nahi khulta — error aa jaata hai.

2) Seller apna wo product DELETE nahi kar pata jis ki tasveerein lagi hon — delete dabane par kuch nahi hota.

3) Seller ke "Balance / Wallet details" wala page nahi khulta — error dikhata hai.

4) Sabse zaroori: jab ek customer "Buy Now" karta hai to baqi customers ke pending cart items galti se delete ho jaate hain.

5) Naya seller jab PEHLI BAAR apni profile photo lagata hai to error aa jaata hai.

6) Bina login kiye bhi kuch pages khul jaate hain aur error dete hain (order place karna, auto-parts add karna, replacement page) — login zaroori honi chahiye.

7) Home page par 10 NAKLI foreign dukanein dikh rahi hain (Paris, Tokyo waghera) jo asli nahi — click karo to khaali dukaan khulti hai. Ye demo data hai, hatana zaroori.

8) Customer "Forgot Password" (password bhool gaye) par click kare to page hi nahi milta.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
🟠 CUSTOMER (khareedar) ki taraf
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

• Dashboard par order ki ginti galat dikhti hai (Active/Completed), aur har order ka status hamesha "Order Placed" hi dikhata hai.

• Wishlist mein product ki qeemat ULTI dikh rahi hai (kam ki jagah zyada wali qeemat).

• Order complete hone par jo order number dikhta hai wo NAKLI hai — us se order track nahi hota.

• Checkout par mulk default "Kuwait" aur payment "KNET" (Kuwait ka system) laga hua hai — Pakistan/Rs. ke liye galat.

• Login kiye customer ki default profile photo tooti hui dikhti hai.

• Footer ka email/newsletter subscribe button kaam nahi karta.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
🟠 SELLER (dukaandar) ki taraf
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

• Product EDIT karte waqt purani tasveer hataye to wo hatti nahi — live rehti hai.

• Seller ko har seller ke har product ka notification chala jaata hai, chahe wo product uska ho ya na ho (notification spam).

• Dashboard ke "Recent Sold" mein product ki chhoti tasveer tooti dikhti hai.

• Dashboard par balance abhi FAKE "999" dikh raha hai (asli balance nahi).

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
🔐 ADMIN / SECURITY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

• Admin login ki security kamzor hai:
   - Login par baar-baar galat password try karne ki koi rok nahi (koi guess kar sakta hai).
   - Ek admin ka password "admin123" bahut aasan hai — badalna chahiye.
   - Login ke baad ka session poori tarah secure nahi hota.

• Product reviews mein koi shararti insaan nuksaan-deh cheez daal sakta hai (security kamzori).

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
🟡 CHHOTI CHEEZEIN (polish)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

• Kuch jagah qeemat "PKR" likhi hai, kuch jagah "Rs." — ek jaisa hona chahiye.
• Kuch links kaam nahi karte: checkout par Terms/Privacy, khaali cart ka "Continue Shopping", header ka "Categories/More" menu.
• Home/header par kuch NAKLI notifications sabko dikh rahe hain ("order #1234 shipped").
• Coming-soon page ka countdown ulta (purani date) chal raha hai.
• Address form mein "Pakistan" option hi nahi hai.
• Checkout par delivery date thodi ajeeb dikhti hai.
