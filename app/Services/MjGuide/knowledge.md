# MJ Cheezain — Website Knowledge Base (for MJ Guide)

## About the website
- MJ Cheezain is a Pakistani multivendor e-commerce platform. Many vendors sell their products through one storefront.
- Main category right now: **Cosmetics**. A second category, **Auto Parts**, is "Coming Soon" (not launched yet).
- Website: https://mjcheezain.com — works great on mobile phones (app-like experience).
- Currency: Pakistani Rupees, always shown as "Rs.".
- Payment method: **Cash on Delivery (COD)** only. Online payment (cards/wallets) is not available yet.

## Official contact
- Customer support email: **support@mjcheezain.com**
- Vendor / seller inquiries email: **sellers@mjcheezain.com**
- Phone number: NOT published yet — it will be announced soon. Until then, email is the official contact channel.
- Contact page: /contact-us

## Accounts — register & login
- One account system with two types: **Customer** (shopper) and **Vendor** (seller).
- To register or login: click the **account/profile icon** in the header, or the login popup that appears — or open the login page directly at **/login-user**.
- Login steps: 1) Open /login-user (or the login popup) 2) Enter your registered email and password 3) Press Login. Customers land on their dashboard; vendors land on the vendor dashboard.
- Signup steps: on the same popup/page switch to Sign Up, fill name, email, password (and account type), then submit.
- Forgot password: use the "Forgot password" link on the login page.
- Common login problems: wrong email/password (re-check spelling), or trying to login before registering. If the problem continues, email support@mjcheezain.com.

## Shopping — how to order
1. Browse from the home page (categories, featured vendors, product sliders), use Search, or open **/products/all** to see all products.
2. Open a product page to see images, price, discount, ratings and description.
3. Press **Add to Cart** (or **Buy Now** to go straight to checkout — login required).
4. Open the cart at **/cart**, review items, then press Checkout (**/checkout**).
5. On checkout: choose/enter your delivery address, payment is Cash on Delivery, add contact details and optional order notes, then place the order.
6. After delivery you can rate the product, or request a return/replacement from My Orders.

## Customer panel (after login)
- **Dashboard** (/customer/dashboard): order stats and recent orders.
- **My Orders** (/customer/orders): every order with per-item status (Order Placed → Processing → Shipped → Delivered, or Cancelled). Buttons: Track (timeline), Cancel, Rate product, Request Return, Request Replacement.
- **Wishlist** (/customer/wishlist): products you saved with the heart icon.
- **Addresses** (/customer/addresses): add/edit delivery addresses and set a default one.
- **Profile** (/customer/profile) and **Profile Settings** (/customer/profile/edit): photo, name, email, phone, birthday, bio.
- **Notifications** (/customer/notifications): order updates and platform messages, grouped by date.
- **Returns**: create a return request from My Orders (choose reason, details, photo) and track it step-by-step on the return tracking page.
- On mobile there is a bottom tab bar for quick navigation between these pages.

## Vendor panel (after vendor login)
- **Dashboard** (/vendor/dashboard): sales stats, balance, top categories.
- **Products** (/vendor/products): view products, add new product (/vendor/products/create), edit or remove.
- **Orders** (/vendor/orders): see and fulfil customer orders, update item status.
- **Withdraw** (/vendor/withdraw) and balance details: request payouts of earnings.
- **Returns** (/vendor/returns) and **Replacements** (/vendor/replacements): review and process customer return/replacement requests.
- **Profile / Store settings** (/vendor/profile, /vendor/profile-edit): store name, details, address, photos.
- To BECOME a vendor: register an account as vendor (signup) — for help email sellers@mjcheezain.com. The Vendor Zone info page is at /vendor-zone.

## Returns & replacements (customer side)
- Both are requested from **My Orders** after an item is delivered.
- Return: choose the item → Request Return → select reason, add details and a photo → submit → track progress on the tracking page (a multi-step timeline up to refund).
- Replacement: choose the item → Request Replacement → describe the issue → submit → track it from the same order.
- Vendors review the request; you get notifications when status changes.

## Ratings & reviews
- After a product is delivered, open My Orders → Rate Product → give stars and an optional written review with photos. Reviews appear on the product page.
